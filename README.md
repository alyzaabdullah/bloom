# BLOOM

**Think first. Then ask the machine.** An AI-literacy practice tool, built for [HerWILL](https://herwill.org).

▶ **Try it:** https://alyzaabdullah.github.io/bloom/

BLOOM teaches learners aged 11–14 to work with AI without handing their thinking over to it. Learners commit to their own answer before seeing the machine's, spot the tells of a confident wrong answer, and judge whether an AI is right or just persuasive. The aim is to make them neither too trusting nor reflexively sceptical.

## Modes
- **You First:** commit to your answer, then see the machine's and decide who's right, because sometimes it isn't.
- **The Tell:** something might be wrong with an answer. Say what kind of wrong.
- **The Forge:** you're given a false claim. Make it believable, then take it apart and tell the truth better. This mode is optional; add `?forge=off` to the link to switch it off.
- **The Arena:** the machine has a plan. Call the flaw.
- **The Mirror:** not a score, but how you handle a confident machine, measured both ways (caving in and digging in).

A 12-item **check-in** and a matched **check-out** measure whether the learner got better at telling sound answers from broken ones, scored as hits and false alarms (d′ and c).

## Privacy
Everything runs in the browser. There are no accounts, no live AI and no server. Progress is kept only on the device. At the end, the learner gets a results code to hand in to their teacher; it does not include their name. Every machine answer is labelled as written by BLOOM's team, not by a live AI.

## Status
Prototype (v2.3). The item bank is authored, and a human proof-read is planned before use with students.
